Bomb pots

Everyone antes, the flop comes out, and the betting starts there. Nobody folds before the flop.

How it plays

  1. Everyone dealt in puts in the same ante. That's the pot. No blinds.
  2. Two cards each, and the flop comes straight out.
  3. Betting opens on the flop. From there it's a normal hand.

Six players ante 10, and the flop lands on a pot of 60 with all six still in. Even the tightest player at the table finally plays a hand.

In nostakes

  • The host turns bomb pots on under Blinds, sets the ante, and picks every so many hands or only when called. Bomb pot next hand in the menu calls one.
  • Short of the ante? You put in what you have, and side pots form before anyone sees a card.
  • No blinds and no straddles. The button and the blinds don't move for it, so a bomb pot never skips your blind or charges you one.
  • Double board: 2 boards, half of every pot on each. Win both and you scoop.
  • Works in Omaha too. A bomb pot pays no seven-deuce bounty.
