Bomb pots

A bomb pot is a hand where every player puts the same amount in before the cards are dealt, the flop comes out at once, and the betting starts on the flop.

There is no betting before the flop, so nobody folds a bad starting hand. Every player sees three cards with a pot already in the middle.

How a bomb pot plays

  1. Everyone dealt in puts in the agreed ante. That is the pot. Nobody posts a blind.
  2. Everyone gets two cards, and the flop is dealt straight away.
  3. The first player left of the button acts first, and the betting goes on from there.
  4. From the flop on, it is an ordinary hand: the turn, the river, and a showdown.

Why groups play them

A bomb pot puts everyone in the same hand. The pot starts several times bigger than usual, and the player who folds most hands gets to play this one. With more players in, it takes a stronger hand to win. A bomb pot is also a way to mark the hour, or the last orbit of the night.

Variations

The ante
Agreed before the night, counted in big blinds. Bigger antes mean bigger pots, and shallower stacks behind them.
When
On a schedule, such as once an orbit, or when someone calls one. Some groups let the player on the button call it, some the host.
Double board
Two boards are dealt and each pot is split between them. nostakes does not deal double-board bomb pots.
Other games
Some groups play bomb pots in Omaha. nostakes deals hold'em.
The blinds
Some groups post the blinds as well as the ante.
The seven-deuce bounty
Some groups that play the seven-deuce game leave bomb pots out of it, because nobody chose to play the hand.

How nostakes runs a bomb pot

Bomb pots are a house rule the host turns on, under Blinds in the house rules or in the setup sheet before the first hand. The host sets the ante in chips, and how often: every so many hands, or only when the host calls one. Either way, the host can call one for the next hand with Bomb pot next hand in the menu, and take it back with Call off the bomb pot.

  • Every seat with chips that is not sitting out is dealt in and posts the ante, whatever blind it owes or waits for.
  • A player with less than the ante puts in all they have. That makes side pots before anyone sees a card. Any part of an ante that no other player could match comes back.
  • Nobody posts a blind and nobody straddles. The bomb pot ante replaces the table's usual ante.
  • The flop is dealt at once. When the board is run more than once, every run shares that flop.
  • A bomb pot has its own button, which moves one seat each bomb pot, so the same player does not get it twice in a row. The first player after it who can act opens the betting on the flop, and the smallest bet is the big blind.
  • The table's button and blinds do not move for a bomb pot, and what each player owes stays the same. A bomb pot cannot skip your blind or charge you one.
  • A bomb pot pays no seven-deuce bounty.

Between hands the table says Bomb pot next hand, and how much everyone antes. During the hand each seat's ante reads Bomb pot and the amount.
