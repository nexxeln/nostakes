The seven-deuce game

Win a pot with 7-2, the worst hand in hold'em, and everyone else pays you a bounty.

How the bounty works

7♣︎ 2♦︎
  1. Agree on the bounty before the first hand.
  2. Get dealt a seven and a deuce. Suits don't matter.
  3. Win the pot with it. A bluff that gets everyone to fold counts.
  4. Show it, and every other player pays you.

Six players and a bounty of 10: you collect 50 on top of the pot. The catch is that every big bet might now be 7-2. Expect bluffs, light calls, and stories.

In nostakes

  • The host turns it on under Showdown and sets the amount. It starts at 10.
  • You're paid when you win, alone, every pot you're in, on every run. A split or a lost run pays nothing.
  • Everyone dealt in pays, folded players too. Short stacks pay what they have.
  • No need to claim it. If nobody saw your cards, the table turns them up for you.
  • Bomb pots and Omaha pay no bounty.
