The straddle

A straddle is an optional blind, usually twice the big blind, that the player to the left of the big blind posts before the cards are dealt.

That seat is called under the gun, or UTG, so this is the UTG straddle. For one hand it doubles the stakes before the flop.

How it plays

At blinds of 1 and 2, the player under the gun straddles 4.

  • The straddle acts like a third, bigger blind. Calling now costs 4, not 2.
  • The first player to act is the one to the left of the straddler.
  • The straddle is live. When the action comes back round, the straddler can check if nobody raised, or raise, just as the big blind could.
  • A raise has to be at least 4 more, making it at least 8.
  • After the flop, the order of play is the usual one.

What it changes

Everyone's stack is worth half as many big blinds for that hand, so the play is looser and the pots bigger. A buy-in of 100 big blinds plays like 50 straddles.

The straddler pays for the right to act last before the flop, then acts early on every later street, which is a weak position. Straddling is a cost on average. People do it for the action, and some groups straddle every hand.

Variations

Re-straddle
The player after the straddler may straddle again, usually double again. Groups set how many are allowed.
Button straddle
The player on the button straddles. The action then starts with the small blind, and the button acts last before the flop as well as after it. Some call it a Mississippi straddle.
Size
Each straddle doubles the one before, or every straddle is the same two big blinds.
Heads up
Some games allow no straddle with only two players.

How nostakes handles straddles

The host decides which straddles the table allows, under Straddles in the house rules. The setup sheet has a switch for one UTG straddle. Straddles are off by default.

UTG straddles
How many players in a row after the big blind may straddle, up to 6. 0 turns it off.
Button straddle
Whether the player on the button may straddle.
Each straddle
Doubles, so 2, 4, then 8 big blinds, or 2 BB every time.
  • Each player chooses for themselves with the Straddle switch in the menu. It stays on hand after hand until they turn it off or leave their seat.
  • At each deal, the player under the gun straddles if their switch is on. Then the next player re-straddles if theirs is on, and so on, up to the number allowed and never past the button. The chain stops at the first player whose switch is off.
  • When the button straddle is allowed and the player on the button has their switch on, the button straddles, if they can cover it, and nobody straddles under the gun that hand.
  • A straddle must leave the player with chips behind. A player who cannot cover theirs does not straddle, and nor does anyone after them in the chain.
  • There is no straddle with only two players dealt in, and none in a bomb pot.
  • The last straddle is live. The player after it acts first, the straddler acts last before the flop, and the smallest raise is the size of that straddle.

The chips posted carry a Straddle tag on the table until the player acts.
