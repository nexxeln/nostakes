The straddle

An optional blind, usually twice the big blind, posted before the deal by the player left of the big blind.

How it plays

That seat is under the gun, so it's the UTG straddle. At blinds of 1 and 2, UTG puts in 4.

  • Calling now costs 4, not 2. The smallest raise makes it 8.
  • The player left of the straddler acts first.
  • The straddle is live: when the action comes back, the straddler can check or raise, like a big blind.

It doubles the stakes for a hand and costs the straddler on average. People do it for the action.

Variations

Re-straddle
The next player straddles again, usually double again.
Button straddle
The button straddles and acts last before the flop too. Also called a Mississippi straddle.
Size
Each straddle doubles the last, or every straddle is 2 big blinds.

In nostakes

  • The host picks which straddles are allowed, under Straddles. They're off by default.
  • You turn on the Straddle switch in the menu. It stays on until you turn it off or stand up.
  • Straddles chain from UTG up to the number allowed, and stop at the first player whose switch is off.
  • When the button straddle is allowed and on, it replaces the UTG straddle.
  • You need chips left behind to straddle. No straddles heads-up or in a bomb pot.
