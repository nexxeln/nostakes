The terms, in short

Last changed 11 November 2026.

nostakes keeps score, nothing more

  • Chips in nostakes are numbers. nostakes never handles, holds, or moves money, and it never takes a cut.
  • If your group settles up for real, that happens between you, outside the app, and it is up to you. Only play for money where that is allowed.

Playing

  • It is for friends. Play with people you know, and be decent.
  • Do not attack the servers, flood them, or automate the app to bother other people.
  • Rooms delete themselves 30 days after the last thing that happened in them. Copy the settlement if you want to keep it.

No promises

  • nostakes is free and comes as it is. We work hard to keep every chip right and the deck checkable, but we cannot promise it will always be up or that a night will never be lost.
  • What we count about how it is used is on the privacy page.
  • These terms may change. The date above says when they last did.
