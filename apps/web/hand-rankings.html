Poker hand rankings

Best to worst: royal flush, straight flush, four of a kind, full house, flush, straight, three of a kind, two pair, one pair, high card.

The ten hands, best first

Make your best 5 cards from your 2 and the 5 on the table. The figure is how often you make it if you see the river.

  1. A♠︎ K♠︎ Q♠︎ J♠︎ 10♠︎

    Royal flush. Ace to ten, all one suit.

    0.003% of the time

  2. 9♥︎ 8♥︎ 7♥︎ 6♥︎ 5♥︎

    Straight flush. Five in a row, all one suit.

    0.03%

  3. Q♠︎ Q♥︎ Q♦︎ Q♣︎ 4♠︎

    Four of a kind. All four of one rank. Quads.

    0.17%

  4. K♠︎ K♥︎ K♦︎ 6♣︎ 6♠︎

    Full house. Three of one rank and two of another. A boat.

    2.6%

  5. A♥︎ J♥︎ 8♥︎ 5♥︎ 2♥︎

    Flush. Five of one suit.

    3.0%

  6. 9♠︎ 8♦︎ 7♥︎ 6♣︎ 5♥︎

    Straight. Five in a row, any suits.

    4.6%

  7. 8♠︎ 8♥︎ 8♦︎ K♣︎ 3♠︎

    Three of a kind. Three of one rank. Trips, or a set with a pair in your hand.

    4.8%

  8. J♠︎ J♦︎ 4♥︎ 4♣︎ A♠︎

    Two pair. Two of one rank and two of another.

    23.5%

  9. 10♠︎ 10♥︎ A♦︎ 7♣︎ 4♠︎

    One pair. Two of one rank.

    43.8%

  10. A♠︎ J♦︎ 8♥︎ 6♣︎ 3♠︎

    High card. Nothing matches. The highest card plays.

    17.4%

Ties and kickers

Same hand? Compare the cards that make it, highest first. Still tied? The other cards in your five are kickers, and they decide.

BoardK♥︎ 9♦︎ 7♣︎ 4♠︎ 2♥︎
YouA♦︎ K♣︎
ThemK♠︎ Q♠︎

Both of you have kings. Your ace beats their queen.

Only 5 cards play. If the board is your best five, everyone still in plays it too, and the pot is split. Suits never break a tie. When an exact tie leaves an odd chip, nostakes gives it to the winner closest to the left of the button.

The mix-ups that start arguments

  • A flush beats a straight. A full house beats both.
  • An ace can be low. A-2-3-4-5 is the lowest straight. Q-K-A-2-3 isn't a straight at all.
    5♣︎ 4♦︎ 3♥︎ 2♠︎ A♣︎
  • Three pairs are still two pair. Play the best two, and the best card left is your kicker.
  • Four to a flush is nothing. It's a draw until the fifth card comes.

Let the table read the hands

nostakes reads every showdown, names the hand, and pays the pot, side pots included. Nobody argues about a kicker. New to this? Play five short hands.
